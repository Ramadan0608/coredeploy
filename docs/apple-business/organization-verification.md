# Organization Verification

Apple Business Manager (ABM) requires organizations to verify their identity before accessing enterprise services such as Automated Device Enrollment, Apps and Books, and Managed Apple Accounts.

Verification helps Apple confirm that the organization is legitimate and that the individual enrolling has the authority to manage Apple services on behalf of the organization.

---

# Audience

- Enterprise Administrators
- IT Managers
- Apple Administrators
- Procurement Teams
- Security & Identity Teams

---

# Prerequisites

Before enrolling in Apple Business Manager, ensure you have:

- A legal business or organization.
- A company email address (avoid personal email accounts).
- Authority to enroll the organization.
- Business registration information available if requested.

---

# Organization Verification Process

## Step 1 – Start Enrollment

Visit:

https://business.apple.com

Select:

> Enroll your Organization

---

## Step 2 – Enter Organization Information

Provide:

- Legal Organization Name
- Country or Region
- Business Address
- Phone Number
- Website
- Organization Type

The information should match publicly available business records.

---

## Step 3 – Verify the Administrator

Apple requires the first administrator to:

- Use a corporate email address.
- Be authorized to sign agreements.
- Accept Apple's Terms and Conditions.

---

## Step 4 – Apple Verification

Apple performs verification using multiple data sources, including public business information and commercial verification providers.

Additional documentation may occasionally be requested.

---

# D-U-N-S Number

## Previous Requirement

Historically Apple required many organizations to provide a Dun & Bradstreet (D-U-N-S®) Number during enrollment.

---

## Current Process

Apple has simplified enrollment for many organizations.

For many supported countries:

- A D-U-N-S Number is **no longer required during enrollment**.
- Apple performs organization verification using its own verification process.
- Additional business information may still be requested.

Requirements can vary depending on:

- Country
- Organization type
- Verification results

Always follow the requirements presented during enrollment.

---

# Countries

Apple continues expanding countries that support streamlined organization verification.

Availability changes over time.

Always verify current availability on Apple's official enrollment page before starting enrollment.

---

# After Verification

Once approved, administrators can configure:

- Managed Apple Accounts
- Identity Federation
- Platform SSO
- Apps and Books
- Automated Device Enrollment
- Locations
- Roles
- Device Assignments

---

# Best Practices

✅ Use a shared IT administration email.

✅ Ensure company information is consistent across public records.

✅ Use your organization's legal name.

✅ Assign at least two Administrators.

✅ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication.

✅ Document administrator ownership.

---

# Troubleshooting

## Organization cannot be verified

Possible causes:

- Incorrect legal organization name.
- Public records are outdated.
- Organization recently registered.
- Verification requires additional documentation.

---

## Administrator rejected

Possible causes:

- Personal email address.
- Insufficient authority.
- Incorrect contact information.

---

## Verification taking longer than expected

Check:

- Email inbox
- Spam folder
- Apple Business Manager notifications

---

# Core Deploy Field Notes

From enterprise deployments, verification is usually completed quickly when:

- The organization's public information is accurate.
- Corporate email addresses are used.
- The administrator is an employee rather than an external consultant.

For multinational organizations, consider enrolling using the legal entity responsible for purchasing Apple devices.

When supporting customers, verify the organization's legal information before beginning enrollment to reduce delays.
